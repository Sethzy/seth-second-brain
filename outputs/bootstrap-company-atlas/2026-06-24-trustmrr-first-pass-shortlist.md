## Early Read

The most Seth-shaped pattern is not generic AI SaaS. It is a narrow operator workflow where the product:

- starts from an existing messy channel like WhatsApp, email, forms, PDFs, Google Maps, public records, or booking pages;
- produces an auditable action or artifact: booked call, scored lead, clean CSV, public-record alert, invoice, appointment reminder, or CRM update;
- can start as a service or concierge workflow;
- is sellable to small businesses, agencies, operators, or vertical professionals without enterprise procurement.

The first deeper pass should compare these clusters against Singapore-starting wedges, not just global category attractiveness.
