# 8 Objections Every AI Services Prospect Raises (and how to destroy them)

## How to use these

These are the eight objections you will hear most when selling an AI service, with a tight one to two sentence response for each. They are scripts, not rules. Read them in your own voice, do not recite them word for word. 

The goal is not to win an argument, it is to reframe the objection so the prospect sees the real cost of staying stuck. 

Lead with the reframe, then stop talking and let them respond. Pick the three or four that match the objections you hear most and rehearse them until you do not have to think about the answer.

---

### 1. "I can just use ChatGPT myself."

You can, the same way you can do your own books with QuickBooks. You are not paying for AI access, you are paying for someone who knows exactly which workflows to fix first and how to make them actually work.

### 2. "AI is overhyped, I'm not convinced it works for my business."

Fair, most AI demos are toys. That is why we start with one concrete win you can see working in days, so you can judge the potential based on real results.

### 3. "It's too expensive."

Look at what you get back, not what you pay. If a few thousand dollars buys you back 5+ hours a week or unlocks revenue you’re currently leaving on the table, the only expensive option is not taking action.

### 4. "I don't have time for this right now."

That is the exact problem we solve, you are too buried to fix the thing keeping you buried. We handle the build so the time comes back, it does not add to your plate.

### 5. "I tried AI before and got garbage results."

Almost everyone does, because they jump straight to building without auditing where AI can actually help most. We run an effort versus impact matrix on your existing workflows first, so we attack the highest leverage opportunities instead of guessing.

### 6. "My business is too specialized for AI."

Every owner says that, and every business still runs on Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, and Intelligence which is exactly where the leverage lives. We map your specific workflows, not a generic template.

### 7. "Won't this replace my team or scare my employees?"

It does the opposite, it pulls the grunt work off your team so they do the job you actually hired them for. We automate tasks, not roles.

### 8. "Let me think about it / I need to talk to my partner."

Totally fair, and here is the real decision: every week you wait is another chunk of hours spent on a task AI could be doing for you. The cost of starting is small, the cost of staying stuck compounds.
