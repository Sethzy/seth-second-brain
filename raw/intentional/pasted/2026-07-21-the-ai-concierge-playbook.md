## Step 2: Send the intake form (your mini audit)

Send this in Jotform before the first call. The rule is simple: **no form, no call.** That form is what lets you show up to call one with a game plan instead of burning the first 30 minutes figuring out where to start.

Make every field required so they cannot submit without giving you ammunition. Here is what to ask:

**Business basics**

- [ ]  What does the business do
- [ ]  Their name and title
- [ ]  Hours spent in the business versus on the business

**Time sinks (your most important early signal)**

- [ ]  What tasks eat the most of their time
- [ ]  Where are they the bottleneck
- [ ]  What balls get dropped when they get swamped

**Process inventory**

- [ ]  One to two repeatable weekly or monthly processes inside each of the five pillars: Sales, Operations, Finance, Marketing, and Intelligence

**Tool stack**

- [ ]  Top 10 software tools they use
- [ ]  AI tools they already pay for and how they use them
- [ ]  Who is on the team and who is already using AI

**Outcomes (the most important section by far)**

- [ ]  If you reclaimed 10 hours per week, what would you do with that time
- [ ]  What does winning look like 90 days from now

**Logistics**

- [ ]  Best email for recordings and recap links
- [ ]  Have they installed Voxer
- [ ]  Anything else I should know

That outcomes section is the whole game. Everything you build points at their 90-day answer. What I keep finding is that the goal they think will take 90 days gets hit by call two or call three, and that is when they see the ROI with total clarity.

## Step 3: Build the Notion Hub

This is the client's home base and your single best retention tool. Give every client their own hub with these sections:

- [ ]  **Quick links:** your Voxer profile, your scheduling link, your email
- [ ]  **Engagement overview:** client name, start date, cadence, primary contact, focus area
- [ ]  **Open action items:** pulled from each call transcript, for you and for them
- [ ]  **Call log:** one entry per call, newest on top
- [ ]  **How this works:** a short reminder of how the engagement runs

Each call log entry includes the date, the duration, a link to the recording in Google Drive, a link to the Fathom summary, the top three takeaways, the action items from that call, and the single most important piece:

**Tools and skills built on this call.** This section spells out in plain detail exactly what you accomplished. Something like "we built two skills and created three context files." That is what the client sees when they open the hub, and it is the most powerful retention mechanism in the entire system.

The best part: a Claude skill fills this entire hub out for you after every call. More on that in Step 5.

## Step 4: Run the first strategy call

You have a paid client on the line and you want to move the needle on call one. There are two tracks.

**Track 1: They are new to Claude Cowork (this is 98% of clients)**

Call one is onboarding them to Cowork. I email them the AOA onboarding plugin at the start of the call, they install it, and it runs them through a guided setup while they share their screen. I just tell them what to click.

That plugin runs the cowork-onboarding skill, which:

- [ ]  Builds their context files (brand voice, about me, voice of the customer, working preferences)
- [ ]  Connects all their tools
- [ ]  Writes their global instructions for Cowork
- [ ]  Runs a lightweight security audit
- [ ]  Schedules a few tasks specific to their workflow

Frame it honestly: this first call is the boring foundation work, and it makes every future call far more effective. Clients get it immediately.

**Track 2: They already use Cowork properly (rare)**

Skip onboarding and run AOA on their biggest bottleneck right away.

**What is AOA?**

It is the framework for fixing and automating any process:

1. **Audit:** Have them share their screen and show you the manual process exactly as it runs today.
2. **Optimize:** Cut the fat. Find the steps that do not need to happen at all.
3. **Automate:** Turn it into a Claude skill, or have them run it in Cowork instead of by hand.

Run AOA on every bottleneck, one at a time, for the life of the engagement.

## Step 5: Run the back-end skills (under five minutes)

Right after the call, you run one orchestrator skill and the admin is done. Run **AI Concierge Post-Call**, the orchestrator that fires the next two skills in parallel:

- **AI Concierge Follow-Up Email:** reads the transcript, pulls the takeaways and everything you built, and drafts a recap email saved to Gmail for one-click send
- **AI Concierge Call Update:** reads the transcript and fills out the entire Notion Hub with takeaways, action items, and the tools and skills you built

Then review at the human checkpoint and hit send. The point of the recap email is speed. You are quantifying exactly what you built and getting it in front of the client within minutes, so they feel the investment paying off before the day is over.

## Step 6: Mind the gap

There are about two weeks between calls. Voxer is how you bridge them.

Message volume is low, so fast responses cost you little and buy a lot of goodwill.

## FAQ

- What if they have not filled out the intake form before call one?
    
    You do not run the call. No form, no call. The form is what lets you arrive with a plan, so protect that rule.
    
- What if a client wants to move faster than two calls a month?
    
    Go weekly if they want, but double the price. Double the calls means double the work, and you should be compensated for it.
    
- What do I actually build on call one if they are brand new?
    
    Four context files, two scheduled tasks on average, their global instructions, and a fully tailored Cowork setup. It is foundation work, and it makes everything after it far more effective.
    
- How do I keep clients from churning?
    
    The "tools and skills built" section of the Notion Hub. Quantify every win in plain language after every call. When the client can see the needle moving, renewing is obvious.
