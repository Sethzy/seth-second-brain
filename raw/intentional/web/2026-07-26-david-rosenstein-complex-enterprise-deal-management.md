# David Rosenstein — Complex Enterprise Deal Management

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7480649411413544960/

## Capture Text

No one taught me complex enterprise deal management, so here are the three key components to getting a deal across the line.

• Commercials/Procurement
• Legal Approvals
• IT/Security Review

After you’re named the vendor of choice, here are the key steps to follow:

1️⃣ Ask, “Historically, how long do each of those stages take?”

Maybe their legal team is extremely slow. Maybe their IT review is very surface level and quick. This’ll ensure you’re not forecasting a deal this month that’ll take 2 quarters to close.

2️⃣ “Can we manage each independently, or is there a specific order we have to follow?”

This is huge. If IT (2 months) has to happen BEFORE legal (also 2 months), you just found out 2+2=4. If they can happen in parallel, you just shrunk your approval process in half.

3️⃣ Write it down WITH your champion

Literally hop on a call, share your screen, and create a plan. Get a redline deadline, initial IT review commits, and write it out with their feedback. Identify what’s realistic and what’s pushing it.

Then, share that co-creates plan with your champion. Bold which items they own.

4️⃣ Check back every 48 hours.

Stay on them like a mother bird caring for babies. Your deal’s on the line.
