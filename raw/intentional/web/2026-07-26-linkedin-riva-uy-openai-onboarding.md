# Riva Uy — Early impressions from OpenAI onboarding

## Verbatim Text

A little update: after 4.5 incredible years, I’ve moved on from Stripe and joined OpenAI.

Stripe gave me far more than I could ever fit into a LinkedIn post: extraordinary experiences, lessons I’ll carry for life, and most importantly, amazing people, many of whom I’m lucky to now call lifelong friends. I’m deeply grateful for the time we shared.

I’ve spent the past two weeks at OpenAI’s company offsite and onboarding, fully immersed in the culture and technology. A few early impressions:

1. The technology is *insanely* good, and somehow that still feels like a massive understatement.
2. The people are remarkable: wildly smart, yet humble, kind, thoughtful, and genuinely fun.
3. Three ideas came up throughout onboarding, consistently and very intentionally:
   - Empower everyone to build. It doesn’t matter whether you’re techy or not. Everyone is a builder. And build we did, several times a day, often one-shotting custom apps between back to back workshops.
   - Give grace. We are all humans.
   - Above all, build AGI that benefits all of humanity.

It’s rare to feel amazed by what already exists while being even more excited, and humbled, by what’s still ahead.

Onward to hyperspeed 🚀
