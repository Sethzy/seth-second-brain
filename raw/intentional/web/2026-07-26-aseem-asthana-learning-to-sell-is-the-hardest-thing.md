## Capture Text

Learning to sell is the hardest thing that I have ever done in my life. And cold-calling is like 100x harder. I secretly keep hoping that the person I am dialing does not pick up (talk about self-sabotaging). The sinking feeling when they actually answer is worse than drowning. But at the end of any conversation that goes longer than 30 seconds (even if there is no next step) the euphoria and adrenaline combo is just crazy. I am high for the next 30 minutes. Is it just me?
