# Gael Tang — Selling Versus Closing A Seven-Figure Enterprise Deal

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gaeltang_the-seven-figure-deal-i-closed-at-a-fortune-share-7481175652155658240-9eAz/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_ios&rcm=ACoAAFklzP8BjEopwFb3X1DpQIoGPgEdps-D5rc

## Capture Text

The seven-figure deal I closed at a Fortune 100 was easier to sell than most small deals.

It just took six months to sign.

The selling was done in six weeks. The buyer got the pain fast. The ROI was obvious. Buy-in came quick.

Then four months vanished into procurement, legal, contract review, compliance.

Selling was easy. Closing was the grind.

Meanwhile a smaller buyer haggles you over a few thousand and questions the value the whole way.

So if you're missing quota, look at your account list.

You might be grinding small deals that are hard to sell and quick to close, when bigger deals sell themselves and only cost you time.

Go bigger.
