# Dave Kellogg — Quantifying the ROI of GTM AI Spend

Headline: Salespeople are like airplanes.
Author: Dave Kellogg
Published: 2026-07-22T16:45:49.361Z

Salespeople are like airplanes. They only make money when they're in the air.

That's one of my favorite expressions about sales. Too many companies value a salesperson's time based on their salary plus the cost of supporting them (their manager, SE, SDR, pipegen, alliances).

While all that costs money, it's actually the wrong metric.
The real cost is opportunity cost.

If an AE closes $1.2M in ARR per year, that's about $600 of ARR per working hour (assuming ~2,000 hours/year).  And remember, your best salespeople don't just hit quota—they crush it. For them, the opportunity cost of an hour may be 2–3× higher.

So when AI, process improvements, or better operations free up an hour, don't ask, "What did that hour cost?"

Ask, "What else could they have done with that hour?"  That's the number that matters.

Hat tip to Mark Roberge, whose recent Topline piece (linked below) on measuring the ROI of GTM AI got me thinking about this distinction and reminded me of that old saying.
