Voice AI just had its biggest funding and model week of 2026. Bland closed a $50M Series C led by Dell Technologies Capital, with Max Levchin, Jeff Lawson, and ElevenLabs CTO Piotr Dąbkowski as angels. The bet that built Bland: in-house voice models, no swapping in OpenAI or Anthropic. 3.5M calls per week, $100M+ raised in under three years. Cartesia shipped Sonic-3.5 (TTS) and Ink-2 (STT) and now holds #1 on both streaming Voice Arena leaderboards. Sonic-3.5 beat ElevenLabs v3, gpt-4o-mini-tts, and Grok TTS across 11,000+ blind listener votes. The only provider that's #1 at both speaking and listening. DeepL acquired Mixhalo and opened its first San Francisco office. Live-event ultra-low-latency audio joins DeepL Voice for conference stages, customer support, and Amazon Connect. CNTXT AI raised a $60M Series A co-led by AI71 and BlueFive Capital, days after acquiring Actualize. Sovereign Arabic voice AI for the GCC, with 250+ enterprise customers and 150K users on Munsit. Speechmatics launched Melia, a multilingual STT model with code-switching across all 55+ supported languages. Beats Deepgram, Microsoft, and AssemblyAI on most FLEURS languages, strongest exactly where most models fail: accented, code-switched speech. Google opened preorders for the Google Home Speaker, the first audio device built for Gemini for Home. $99.99, ships June 25, runs local models for noise cancellation and sound separation. Gemini becomes ambient. Krisp launched Voice Security with real-time Deepfake Detection and Agent Voice Verification for contact centers. Krisp already processes 12B+ minutes of voice agent traffic per year across 130 products. Fraud now gets caught at the audio layer, before transcription. Vonage launched API Power for Kiro, the first CPaaS available natively inside AWS Kiro's agentic IDE. Millions of AWS developers can now wire Vonage voice and verification APIs into agent code via MCP. MiniMax shipped Speech 2.6 with sub-250ms latency and Fluent LoRA for cleaning imperfect voice clones, and the model now ships natively inside Retell 's voice agent builder. Retell also rolled out a major ASR upgrade: 22+ new languages including Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Tamil, Swahili, and Hebrew, bringing total coverage past 50. Mandarin and Hindi WER dropped 15-25 points. AssemblyAI upgraded Universal-3 Pro (19% multilingual WER reduction) and expanded its LLM Gateway to unify 20+ models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Baseten with cross-provider fallback. Speech-to-action middleware is starting to look like AWS for voice. Inworld AI 's Realtime API now plugs into Mastra. One WebSocket, one constructor argument, full-duplex speech-to-speech with semantic VAD, barge-in, and tool calling. Capital concentrated. The leaderboard reshuffled. Sovereign and multilingual stacks claimed their week.
