# Gael Tang — Pick better accounts

## Verbatim Text

Every new company, I ask the top reps the same question: what do you wish someone told you on day one?

The answer is almost always the same.

Pick better accounts.

The rep closing a million a year isn't working 10 times harder than the rep closing 100K. They're not smarter either.

They spend their limited hours on the accounts most likely to pay off, and they say no to the rest.

You can't chase every deal in the market.

You can only chase the right ones.

How much of your pipeline right now is there because you were strategic, versus because it said yes to a meeting?
