# Kyle Asay — Four times more reps hitting quota

## Verbatim Text

We’ve more than 4xed the number of reps hitting quota in my org over the last year. We’ve had teams with 100% of reps exceeding quota.

3 key changes helped us move in the right direction:

1) Effective pipeline generation plans

We went from reps wondering “what PG should I do today?” to an organized and focused plan ensuring we generated quality pipeline every week.

You can grab my PG Plan template here: https://lnkd.in/g9sH7Vjq

2) First call preparation and execution

We went from superficial “feature-based” discovery to uncovering business priorities we can solve better than our competition.

You can grab my Discovery Blueprint here: https://lnkd.in/gT7ZMQQz

3) Territory management

We went from working all accounts evenly to spending 80%+ of our time in our “most likely to win” accounts.

You can grab my Territory Framework here: https://lnkd.in/gV6uEuRs

I’m grateful to my team for the incredible effort they’ve put in to drive improvement. I’m lucky to work with them. I do my best to offer coaching/training to support their success, but they are the ones putting in the effort every day to keep our org moving the right direction.

And it’s incredible to see our momentum grow each quarter.
