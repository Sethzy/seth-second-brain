# Nate Nasralla — Three phrases for stuck deals

## Verbatim Text

Here are 3 phrases from Jen Allen-Knuth's workshop last week that I LOVE, and have worked time and time again for our sellers.

Because chances are, you've got at least 1 deal that's stuck right now, it's EOQ, and you're trying to figure out what to do next. Without "just checking in."

So please try them. (They're all built around the customer's POV and goals, vs. ours.)

_____

(1) The Unexpected Hurdle:

An email that literally just says, "Hey, did we hit an unexpected hurdle on [ project ]?"

That's it, to give them an easy opening to tell you what's really going on.

____

(2) The New Idea:

"Saw the news re: [ X ], and did some digging and it looks like [ Y ]. Had a couple ideas based on [ Z ]. Open to talking through those?"

I use this one more than any other: It works, but it takes work (you have to actually create something that gets their job done, even before the buy: a customer intro, a redacted deliverable, a framework, etc).

And it shifts you from that "rep who needs a deal," to "OMG, I love getting their emails, they keep showing up with something useful."

____

(3) Elephant in the Room / Rookie Mistake:

"I was thinking about our last conversation, and realized I made a rookie mistake…"

@null did this last week with a July deal, to open a meeting and the customer LOVED it.

When we take ownership of what we could've done better, there's always grace.

____

Any other phrases or emails for stuck deals you like using, instead of "just checking in?"
