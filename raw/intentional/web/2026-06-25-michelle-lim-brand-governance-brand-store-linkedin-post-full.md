## Public LinkedIn Post Text

I chatted with two enterprise CMOs recently ($300m+ ARR companies). Both of them are looking to solve the same problem:

Brand governance.

An example:

SEO, ABM, product marketing, product managers, and engineering teams are all building externally-facing pages independently 

Teams are making pages in silos, with zero visibility into what anyone else is making.

Both of them had a clear vision for what the solution should look like: a brand store.

A central repository of approved brand assets, with reusable campaign/page/ad building blocks.

What they liked about Flint was that this model already exists.

Flint has a brand store we use to generate all pages. You bootstrap it by giving Flint your website URL -> Watch how we generated all the pages for Tandem using just their homepage brand as a guide.

Sure, you could piece together page generation with Claude + Vercel - that part's easy.

The hard part is everything around it: brand consistency across teams, shared templates, QA before publishing.

Brand is an infrastructure layer most teams underestimate building.
