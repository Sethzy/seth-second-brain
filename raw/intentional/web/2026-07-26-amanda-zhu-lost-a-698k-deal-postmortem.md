# Amanda Zhu — Lost a $698K Deal Postmortem

## Capture Text

Headline: I lost a $698K deal.
Author: Amanda Zhu (hiring ex-founders)
Published: 2026-07-16T15:05:01.780Z

I lost a $698K deal. This is a breakdown of our 30-minute postmortem and what’s changed since.

The prospect was a fast growing sales tool company.
Their CTO was eager.

Four of his engineers joined the follow-up call.
They gave us their current volume and their projected 12 month volume.
The CTO asked for pricing.

I gave a price.
He said no.
And then ghosted.

Later that week we ran the postmortem over lunch.
Pulled the deal up on the TV.

Three people who worked on the deal.
Two people who had nothing to do with it.

The outsiders asked the questions we couldn’t ask ourselves:
”What did you price on?”
”Did he ever say the number was too high, or did he just go quiet?”
”Did you offer a ramp?”

Twenty minutes in, we distilled it down to one thing: I anchored on the number they’d hit in 12 months instead of the number they were at today.

The projected pricing was accurate.
But sticker shock.

I now include someone from outside every deal in every postmortem.
The best question in a debrief is the one nobody on the deal would think to ask.
