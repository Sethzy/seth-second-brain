Headline: One of my former Sales leaders had a rule for deal reviews.
Author: Jen Allen-Knuth
Published: 2026-07-22T18:04:53.944Z

One of my former Sales leaders had a rule for deal reviews. If I couldn't answer these 3 Qs about a deal, I wasn't allowed to bring it to a deal review:

What do they sell? 
Who do they sell it to? 
How do they sell it? 

I remember the first time I had a garbage answer to the first question. 

"They're in manufacturing." 

"That wasn't my question. What do they sell?"

I turned red and admitted I didn't know, expecting that he would explain it to me. 

"Who's up next?"

As I watched the next AE go, I realized why these Qs were so important. 

Context. 

When I didn't understand what a company sold, to whom, and how, my Sales conversation was me pitching our solution, and leaving it to the prospect to figure out if/how it fits. 

When I knew the answers to those 3 Qs, I was able to build a POV that showed I attempted to understand how the problem our solution solved might be playing out in their world. 

This allowed me to build a "so what" story upfront. 

My boss wasn't being hard on us. He was teaching us that context matters, and holding us accountable for doing the work.
