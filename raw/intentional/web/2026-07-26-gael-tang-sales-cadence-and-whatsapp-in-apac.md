# Gael Tang — Sales Cadence and WhatsApp in APAC

## Capture Text

Headline: The purpose of a sales cadence is to get out of it.
Author: Gael Tang
Published: 2026-07-17T02:00:10.365Z

The purpose of a sales cadence is to get out of it. Fast.

Most reps do the opposite: they live in it, blasting email.

Sales engagement platforms were never built to mass-mail your contact list. They're built to run outbound across channels, repeatably.

The job of a cadence is to find the one channel your buyer actually answers on, and move the conversation there.

In Southeast Asia that channel is usually WhatsApp. Indonesia, Singapore, India: the deal happens there, not in an inbox.

Sell into India on email alone and you'll wait forever. Nobody reads it.

Stop measuring cadences by emails sent. Measure them by how fast you get to a real conversation.
