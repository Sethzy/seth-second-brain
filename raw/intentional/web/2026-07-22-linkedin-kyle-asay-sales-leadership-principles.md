# Kyle Asay — Three sales-leadership principles

## Verbatim Text

After 8+ years in sales leadership I know VERY little. But I have developed 3 foundational principles I’ll never abandon:

1) Real life > work

We are not our jobs. Our titles are not our identities. Our career progress matters less than our relationships. I refuse to live a life I’ll regret - I won’t prioritize professional progress over health or family.

And I’m vocal about this so my team knows I support them doing the same.

2) I refuse to take myself too seriously

I make jokes in zoom chat, slack, in posts/comments on LinkedIn. Sometimes they hit, sometimes they suck (I guess I’m a “high volume” guy).

I’m sure there are plenty of CEOs that see my jokes and think “I would NEVER hire that guy to run my sales org - he’s not taking this seriously enough.”

Good - we wouldn’t work well together. I’ll stick with leadership that loves how we can deliver numbers AND have fun along the way.

3) You aren’t better than anyone - but you aren’t worse, either

It’s human nature to compare (I’m often guilty of this).

When I fall into this trap I try to remind myself: “you aren’t better than anyone, but you aren’t worse, either.”

It’s easy to feel like a failure when you compare yourself to people “ahead of you.” It’s a debilitating and unproductive exercise.

It’s easy to feel superior when you compare yourself to people “behind you.” That arrogance will destroy your humanity.

I’m happiest when I run in my own lane, help others along their lane, and don’t worry about “who is ahead.”

And I bet you would be too.

Maybe in 8 more years I’ll have learned enough to say “here’s what works in sales leadership.” But for now, this is what I’ve got.
