# David Arnoux — Presence and the shift from seats to task execution

## Verbatim Text

HubSpot fell 12.7% after OpenAI launched Presence.

OpenAI launched Presence, an enterprise platform for deploying voice and chat AI agents across customer support, sales, and internal IT workflows.

The market reaction was fast:

→ HubSpot down 12.7%
→ Atlassian down 11.8%
→ Workday down 9.9%
→ Salesforce down 7.7%

OpenAI also shared that Presence already handles 75% of inbound calls on its own support line without human intervention.

Enterprise software value is moving from application seats to direct task execution.

Software buyers do not want to pay per seat for tools where teams spend hours manually clicking through software UIs.

When an agent executes the task directly through APIs, the SaaS interface never opens.

If your GTM strategy relies on selling user seats rather than outcome automation, buyers will replace your software with direct agent workflows.

The seat-based SaaS model is dying.

Your UI is no longer the product.

Your API is.

Your data model is.

Your connector layer is.

Actionable step for this week:

Audit your product or GTM stack today.

Pick the single workflow where your buyers buy the most seats just to do manual data entry or triage.

Build an API connector that lets an AI agent run that workflow end-to-end.

Shift your pricing conversation to value per completed task before your customers ask for it.

Are your buyers asking for direct API access over seat licenses yet?
