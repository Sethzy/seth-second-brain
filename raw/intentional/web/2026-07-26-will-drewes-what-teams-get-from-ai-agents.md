I kept hitting the same problem in client discovery calls: a buyer asks what AI agents are delivering in their industry, and most of what exists is vendor marketing with no numbers behind it.

So I started keeping a record. The Agent Ledger is a running log of real, measured AI agent outcomes in regulated and high-stakes industries. Named companies. Real figures. A source on every claim. And no vendor names, because I am not in the business of promoting anyone's platform.

First entries: what AI fraud detection actually delivered at PSCU, American Express, and PayPal ($35M in prevented fraud losses over 18 months is the headline number), and what HSBC, Barclays, and Banco do Brasil measured on AML false positives.

New entries land every week, organized by industry. If you want to know what good looks like in your sector before you commit budget to an AI project, that is what it is for. Link in the comments.
