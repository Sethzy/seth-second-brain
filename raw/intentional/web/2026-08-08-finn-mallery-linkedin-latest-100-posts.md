Social media isn’t social anymore. 
It’s just… media. 

Social media has moved from engaging social interactions to passive content consumption. 

Think about the “social” landscape right now: 

Instagram: creators on beaches. 
TikTok: creators dancing. 
YouTube: creators talking. 
Twitter: creators arguing. 
Snap Inc.: dying. 
Facebook: dead. 

This leaves a big gap in truly social, social media: 

People still have a basic human need to meaningfully connect. With their friends, friends of friends, and communities.

And that shift is happening now. 

The perfect example of this is Discord’s acquisition of Gas last month. The Gas app is made for highschoolers, where users can share anonymous compliments and affirmations for their classmates. 

Gas was a top app on the App store and attracted more than 1M DAU and 30,000 new users per hour by the end of last year. 

Why? It was about “gassing up” your friends. A positive place for small communities to share, connect, and play. 

Discord gets it: the future of Social is actually a throwback to the past: sharing, laughing, communicating, FUN. 

The next-gen of Social looks and feels a lot more like the familiar social media of the past:

BeReal.: once-a-day dual-camera, unfiltered photos to share what you’re doing (OG: Instagram)
Gas: high-school community-based anonymous polling (OG: YikYak)
Fizz:  a private news feed & forum for your campus (OG: Reddit, Inc.)

Next-gen social is about micro-communities, authenticity, and fun. 

The big social platforms are no longer set up (or incentivized) to satisfy that particular need. 

Next time you open up Instagram, take note of how it makes you feel. You probably feel like crap after a good long scroll. 

That feeling will perpetuate as “social” media pushes you to consume vs. create. 

And that crap-feeling? IMO, that’s going to move markets.
