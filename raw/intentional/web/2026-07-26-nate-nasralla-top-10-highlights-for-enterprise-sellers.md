If you're early in your sales career, I truly can't think of a better use of your time today, than to soak up some words from Dave Kellogg's writing.

I've read nearly every new blog from him over the past 3 years, and while he largely writes to CEOs, his writing is *wildly* practical for enterprise sellers.

Because a'lotta what you'll read on LinkedIn tells you how to sell. 

But if you look at Kellblog, you'll hear more of what an internal and exec-level convo actually sounds like (from the rooms you're not in), while understanding how those same execs think and operate. 

There's like ~800 posts on his site, I've read probably 95 - 100(ish), and cherrypicked 10 with some notes to get you started. 

With sources for each, if you want to nerd out a little more.

Dave, thanks for your words, I know it's a labor of love 🫡
