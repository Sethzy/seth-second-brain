# Daniel Wax — A self-writing CRM with human approvals

## Verbatim Text

Our sales team hasn't opened HubSpot in 13 days and the data has never been more accurate.

Here's how we did it in case you want to steal it:

The old day in the life of a seller was simple. Sell, then lose an hour a day updating the CRM or lose track of follow ups. We removed the second half at Virio by making the record write itself.

1. Deals create themselves. A meeting gets booked, and the deal shows up in the pipeline with the contact and company attached. This morning it flagged five meetings on tomorrow's calendar with no deal behind them. Sent our team a Slack message about it then created the deals.
2. The record enriches itself. Before anyone looks at the deal, an agent runs the company and contact through Exa and pulls the context we used to go find. It updates on the deal in HubSpot.
3. Fields fill from the call. Sybill knows our pipeline configuration and deal fields. An agent reads every call recording and updates CRM fields when a call surfaces something new. Amount, goals, pains, close date, etc.
4. Slack is the control panel. The agent posts every write it wants to make: one channel for new deals and one for stage moves. Deal owner is tagged in message and a single reply approves it. All thru a quick text or voice note.
5. It verifies before it writes. Before any stage move, the agent pulls the real deal from HubSpot and confirms the ID and current stage. An AI once handed us a clean summary with a deal ID that did not exist. We had to fix that.
6. Every stage has a tiny required set. The two or three fields that earn that stage in our pipeline. The agent fills them so the bar is identical on every deal. All other nice-to-have info is filled as well but not required.

The role of an AE collapsed back to one thing. Talk to buyers (and message in Slack a little).

Right now everything runs off call recordings for context. The next step is pulling from wherever the conversation lives. Email, Slack, Linkedin, etc.

Every move still gets a human approval today. I'm hesitant to make it fully autonomous.

Who else is playing around building like this? Lmk what's been best.

P.S. excited to play around with the new Agent Hub in HubSpot.
