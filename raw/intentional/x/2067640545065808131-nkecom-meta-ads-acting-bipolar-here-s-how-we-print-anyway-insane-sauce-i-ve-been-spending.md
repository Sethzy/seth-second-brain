## Verbatim Text

Meta ads acting bipolar? Here's how we print anyway (insane sauce)

I’ve been spending MILLIONS on Meta every single year since I was 18 years old (I’m 25 now).

I understand how stressful bad performance can be. I've been there.

I’m currently on a flight to Paris to shoot for my brand, so I thought I’d write this banger of piece to post when I land.

I fucking WISH someone told me all this shit before I had to spend 8 years figuring it all out myself.

I’m going to give you the exact two-step strategy I follow whenever our ad accounts are in the red (spoiler: it's not even about cost caps).

I'm calling it the bipolar strategy (because the way to respond to Meta when it becomes bipolar is: focusing on its poles).

Btw, no guru has ever spoken about this because they’re either:

(a) not in the trenches, 
(b) they pretend shit never goes wrong
(c) they have no clue what they are doing

When you’ve been in the game as long as I have - you need to learn how to attack (yes, attack is the right word) turbulent periods on Meta.

Or your whole business suffers & your bank account keeps going down.

Before I give you the full game-plan, first I want to highlight where everyone goes wrong.

During bipolar periods on Meta, the first question people always ask is:

## “Is Meta only this bad for me - or is for everyone?”

And honestly it’s a valid question to have. I used to wonder the same thing (which is actually why I started using cost caps)...

But here’s the problem with that question.

Your subconscious has already determined the answer the second you thought about asking it.

Humans love to externalise blame.

As soon as you give your brain a way out, your subconscious mind will reframe reality until it manifests.

That’s why it’s so easy to see other people complaining about the same thing & automatically default to putting all the blame on Meta.

You are lost in the echo-chamber.

But what if the problem wasn’t Meta?

Or even better:

## What if there really was an issue with Meta BUT YOU HAVE THE POWER TO PRINT ANYWAY.

This is what me I’ve been trying to figure out for years.

So here’s my most up-to-date guide to grabbing Meta by the balls & turning your ad account around.

THE MOST IMPORTANT THING to be aware of in this whole discussion is what Meta being broken actually means.

Because here’s the thing. Every single time you serve an ad - YOU HAVE TO REMEMBER - there is another person on the other side.

Every ad you serve, it goes to a HUMAN.

A Meta glitch does not mean Meta stops consumers from buying.

There is only really ONE LOGICAL EXPLANATION to why your conversion rate is dropping 30-50%...

## It’s because Meta is showing your ads to the WRONG PEOPLE.

So how do we show our ads to the right people?

Here’s where my two-step framework comes into it.

First, you have to force your ad spend to the right people - by going after the lowest hanging fruit.

How do we do this?

Every single one of our brands has a campaign set up where ALL ADS go to the PDP.

The campaign is FULL of bottom of funnel ads.

100s of offer-centric statics & testimonial style vids, all hammering bottom of funnel audiences.

The reason this is so important is because even when Meta’s machine is struggling to find “new audiences” - it should NEVER STRUGGLE in finding people lower down your funnel.

Meta knows exactly who has been visiting your website & watching all your ads. All the data belongs to them.

## Therefore, as soon as our performance drops - the first thing we do is deploy 100+ new BOF static ads, followed by a dozen+ videos THE VERY NEXT DAY.

After that, we INCREASE THE BUDGET on this campaign & reduce the budgets on some of our under-performing, higher funnel campaigns.

By the way, if I ever mention “dropping budgets” - a lot of the time I mean tightening cost caps

Once we have strengthened our BOF campaigns - the south pole - the next phase is where things gets really interesting (can you guess what the next step is?)

## WE THEN SHIFT ALL OUR FOCUS THE COMPLETE OPPOSITE END OF THE FUNNEL - THE NORTH POLE.

We blast tf out of upper funnel ads.

By upper funnel, I mean we create ads for extremely cold, unaware audiences.

Because think about it.

If Meta is temporarily struggling to serve your ads to the right people - surely the biggest solution is to focus on ads that can convert colder traffic.

Then performance becomes less reliant on the algorithm & more reliant on our creatives.

The way in which we do this is by producing fresh, unaware (or problem aware) ads along with fresh landers.

There is a very specific creative-funnel structure we follow here, but honestly, it’s way too much sauce to be giving away on here - but  if you think deeply about everything in this section, you may be able to figure it out yourself.

## The beauty of following this strategy is that BOTH OF THESE CAMPAIGNS WORK IN PERFECT HARMONY within the Meta ECOSYSTEM.

Because once we have solidified our bottom-of-funnel foundations, we then  blast as much top-of-funnel traffic into that ecosystem as possible.

It's what Meta wants (read the GEM papers)...

In reality, you should be doing this shit anyway in your ad account. But I guarantee most of the people who complain about Meta probably aren’t.

Even if you think your ads are top of funnel - they probably aren’t.

## There are so many brands on Meta that are simply middle-of-funnel snipers, built on weak foundations (bof) with no scalability (tof).

Meta's algorithm should boost your brand, not carry it. If your middle of funnel creatives are reliant on Meta's algorithm, don't be surprised when your performance tanks as soon as the algo becomes unstable.

Whenever my ecom friends come to me & complain about theirtad performance (a lot of them are high level), the first thing I do is I check their ad library - 99% of the time, they have this exact funnel problem I’m talking about - or they have a problem that can be solved with cost caps (but thats an article for another day)…

The truth is this;

Yes, Meta can sometimes be rocky,

Yes, it can happen to lots of people simultaneously,

BUT THERE WILL ALWAYS BE PEOPLE PRINTING.

So you need to do everything in your power to be part of that group.

Some of the best life advice I ever received was:

## “There are two things you should never worry about in life: things you can control, and things you can’t”

You will never be able to control Meta’s delivery system - so don’t sit there like a cuck & watch it fuck your brand.

INSTEAD, GRAB IT BY THE BALLS & FOCUS ON WHAT YOU CAN CONTROL.

You can’t control exactly who sees your ad. But you can control the ads you put into the auction & where the traffic goes to.

So just focus on that.

And as I said - the best thing to do is create ads where you aren’t over-reliant on Meta’s MOF-focused targeting system.

Instead, focus on ads that are easier for Meta to deliver.

The best part is, once Meta’s algorithm stabilises - think how much better your performance will be now you have completely dialled in on the most important parts of your funnel...

Everything I talk about on this app is shit we’ve battle-tested.

This strategy is EXACTLY WHAT WE’VE BEEN DOING for one of our brands in the past few weeks.

Because our conversion rate plummeted for the first time.

But guess what... after locking tf in on ultra-TOF (& BOF) ads, performance has never been better.

I really do hope this article helps you.

Because I understand how important Meta performance is & I wish someone told me about this shit before I figured it all out 8 years later.

GOOD LUCK & HAPPY SCALING.
