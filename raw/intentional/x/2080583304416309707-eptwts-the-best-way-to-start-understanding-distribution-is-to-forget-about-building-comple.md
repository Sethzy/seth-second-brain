## Verbatim Text

the best way to start understanding distribution is to forget about "building" completely...

95%+ of indie-hacking X is broke because they obsess over building - their idea of distribution is documenting their journey on X & posting memes on tiktok

this is not the right order of things

instead i'd recommend finding a product that has already proven to sell, grabbing an affiliate link & experimenting w/ the following forms of distribution:

make youtube videos about it, learn about buying intent, learn how to structure your videos like VSL's that convince the viewer to buy - frame the product as the solution of the problem the video is intended to fix

create an email list, use educational shortform content to drive people to it, build up an audience around the products you affiliate for

X is a much more sophisticated market so i wouldn't bother marketing on here until you're very experienced and have valuable insights to share

the irony of this is you won't even have to sell anything to make a living once you get these distribution channels up...

if you do this right you'll start getting brand deals - a channel i cofounded recently has 3k subs & is getting offered $4k per video right now to simply talk about products
