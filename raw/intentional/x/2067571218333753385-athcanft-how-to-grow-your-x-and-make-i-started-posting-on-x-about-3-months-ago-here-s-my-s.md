## 1. PROVIDE VALUE (MUST)

my "niche" is mobile apps, i post about what i've learned, how to run ads, how to optimize the paywall, etc.

people follow me and engage with my posts because i provide VALUE to them

if you just post memes, or random thoughts with no hook, story or result, people are just going to scroll - because it's not interesting

whatever your niche is, your skill, your interest - it's likely you have a lot of knowledge on it - and that there are plenty of people on X seeking for that knowledge - give it to them! and in return - you get engagement

it's quite easy to grow on X when you understand this

---

## 2. THE "HOOK"

the principle of the "hook" on a tweet is exactly the same for any other form of advertising - the first line needs to appeal to the reader and convince them to keep reading

in my niche (mobile apps / online money) examples of hooks that work are:

- "i've made $10k this month..."

- "how i get 8% install-to-paid..."

- "3 ways to maximize your app revenue..."

they work because they provide a very clear numeric statistic that hooks the reader into wanting to know more - because they can clearly see that there may be some value in it for them if they keep reading

even this article's title "how to grow your X (and make $$$)" is using this strategy - and it's clearly working!

here's an example of one of my more viral posts (article) - it clearly states a $ amount ($10K) and emphasises the *ease* of it ("lazymaxxing") - this combination causes intrigue and click throughs

compare that to this post:

nobody cares - what's in it for them? there's no value to be extracted here, so they'll just scroll

## 3. TRY EVERY FORMAT

try to post between 5-10 times a day - videos, articles, single tweets, thread tweets - they're all ways to repackage your knowledge into different formats

every time i post a video, it performs well - i don't do any editing or polishing, just raw footage - it seems X pushes videos quite a bit

not EVERY post needs to be a high effort banger either - the game is volume - put some effort into some posts, for other posts just word vomit - some posts go viral completely randomly and as long as you keep posting, you will eventually get a hit
