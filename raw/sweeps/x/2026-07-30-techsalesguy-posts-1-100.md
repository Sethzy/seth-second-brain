Working through lunch is a major hack to accelerate your career, especially early on.

This was one of the main things I did for a long time to get ahead of everyone else.

While everyone else was at lunch, I ate at my desk for 15 minutes and went right back to it. That extra 45 to 60 minutes really compounds overtime.
