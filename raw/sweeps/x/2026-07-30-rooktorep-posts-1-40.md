I’ve written about this before, but email has a special quality that other outbound channels don’t: 
It gives prospects something tangible to come back to. 
You might not get many responses from VP or leadership-level people, but that doesn’t mean they aren’t reading your emails. 
I recently had a VP-level prospect respond to an email I sent back in March. 
He basically said, “Hey, this is relevant now. We’re evaluating other tooling,” and then gave me the right contacts to speak with. 
He definitely didn’t have my phone number saved. We weren’t connected on LinkedIn.  But the email was still sitting there for him to search, revisit, and continue the conversation when the timing changed.

Your subject line, your company, and your original message all give the prospect a place to come back to when the problem becomes relevant.

Big believer in playing the long game in sales.

There’s no need to force something - that’s usually the easiest way to kill it.

It makes a massive difference and is one of the best ways to build real relationships within an account.
