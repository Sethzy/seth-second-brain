Pre-sales engineer at IBM highlights an industry wide shift that we're starting to see - orgs are shifting technical talent from pre-sales to post sales. Think sales engineer, GTM engineer, solution consultant.  Some quick reactions:

1. As implementations become more complex and AI is embedded across products, this type of talent will need to support the adoption and future customer growth opportunities.

2. This could be a bear case on new logo acquisition but a bull case on NRR.

3. Traditional Account Managers (and CS) may not have the technical chops to support this kind of work (forward deployed engineer sort of thing).

4. Companies will to be mindful of margins as shifting this resource post-sale really should also push it into COGS, so combined with point 3 it could put pressure on AM role. 

5. Account Executives may need to drift towards being more technical in the future if these resources more limited after being shifted to post-sale.

6. With more consumption and usage based pricing, it's imperative to the top line to shift this talent to capture it.
